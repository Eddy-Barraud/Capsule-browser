# Privacy Policy (SilicIA)

**Effective date:** 30 August 2026

Capsule Browser is engineered from the ground up with a strict privacy-first principle:

User-Private iCloud Storage: Your web apps, preferences, and session data are stored securely in your own private Apple iCloud database via CloudKit. The developer has zero access to your saved data, browsing activity, or personal information.

No Third-Party Tracking or Telemetry: The app does not embed third-party advertising SDKs, profiling tools, or tracking servers. The developer only receives standard, anonymized crash reports and technical diagnostics provided directly by Apple if opted in via your Apple device settings.

Isolated Web Containers: Every web app you add runs in its own isolated WebKit container with a non-persistent, sandboxed data store. Cookies, local storage, and caches are restricted and do not leak between apps.

Native WebKit Security: It leverages Apple's native WebKit engine for rendering, inheriting robust, up-to-date system-level security protections
